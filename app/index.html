    About this guide

    Trophy names, descriptions and grades as published by Exophase, PowerPyx, GTABase and PSNProfiles; Rockstar has published no official trophy list. The guides are written in the author’s own words and cite their sources; they do not copy guide text. Two secret trophies are still unconfirmed: Bad Luck, I Guess and Ten Pin. No tracker confirms their unlock method yet, so this guide gives their descriptions only and invents no method.

    Rockstar-confirmed facts used here: released 19 November 2026 on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S only (no PC version announced); set in the state of Leonida including Vice City; protagonists Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos; six regions: Vice City, Leonida Keys, Grassrivers, Port Gellhorn, Ambrosia and Mount Kalaga; single-player. There is no GTA Online at launch and no official trophy list from Rockstar.
